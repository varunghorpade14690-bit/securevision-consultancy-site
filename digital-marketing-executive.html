Digital Marketing Executive

Full-time | Hybrid | Location: Ireland

Apply Now

Role Overview

Securevision is looking for a Digital Marketing Executive to strengthen our online presence, generate qualified leads, and improve how our services are positioned across search, social, and content channels. This role is suited to early-career marketers who can combine campaign execution with clear reporting and a practical understanding of customer engagement.

You will work across SEO, paid and organic campaigns, website content coordination, and channel performance analysis. The role offers exposure to a growing consultancy environment where marketing activity is closely tied to business development and measurable outcomes.

Responsibilities

  • Plan and execute digital campaigns across search, social media, email, and display channels.
  • Support SEO and website visibility initiatives, including keyword research, content updates, and landing page coordination.
  • Manage and maintain Securevision's social media presence with platform-appropriate content and posting schedules.
  • Track campaign performance, prepare reports on ROI and KPIs, and recommend optimizations based on insights.
  • Assist with content creation for service pages, promotional posts, lead-generation assets, and campaign visuals.
  • Coordinate with internal teams to improve user experience and conversion performance across digital touchpoints.
  • Research audience trends, competitor activity, and new growth opportunities relevant to the security and surveillance sector.

Required Experience & Skills

  • 1-3 years of experience in digital marketing, campaign execution, or online brand growth.
  • Working knowledge of SEO, SEM, social media campaigns, email marketing, and digital reporting.
  • Comfort with campaign performance analysis, KPI tracking, and optimization based on data.
  • Strong written communication and the ability to support clear, professional marketing content.
  • Good teamwork, planning, and time management skills in a fast-moving environment.

Preferred Qualifications

  • MSc or equivalent qualification in Digital Marketing, Marketing Analytics, or a related discipline.
  • Experience with Google Ads, Facebook or Instagram campaigns, and search advertising.
  • Exposure to Canva, Adobe Photoshop, landing page collaboration, or marketing asset coordination.
  • Confidence working with customer-facing teams and translating business needs into campaign actions.

What We Offer

  • Hands-on ownership of real campaigns tied to business growth and service visibility.
  • Cross-functional exposure to sales, operations, and client-facing consultancy work.
  • Practical learning in a growing Irish business with room to build a specialist marketing track.
  • A hybrid working model with structured support and clear performance expectations.
